Switching applicant tracking systems shouldn't mean starting from zero. We don't have a self-serve import yet. Instead, we run every migration together with your team, so nothing gets lost on the way over.

## Reach out to start

<Card title="Email the team" icon="mail" href="mailto:support@needle.so?subject=ATS%20migration">
  Write to [**support@needle.so**](mailto:support@needle.so?subject=ATS%20migration). If your team shares a Slack channel with us, post there instead.
</Card>

## What to include

- **Your current ATS**, for example Greenhouse, Ashby, Lever, Workable, or Personio.
- **What you want to bring over:** open jobs, candidates, pipeline stages, notes and feedback, or your careers page.
- **Your timeline**, including any date your current contract ends.
- **Who on your team** we should work with.

## What happens next

1. We set up a short call to agree on what moves and in what order.
2. We plan the move around your hiring, so live roles keep running while it happens.
3. You check the result in Needle before you switch off the old system.

Not sure yet whether to switch? Email us anyway. We're happy to talk it through first.
