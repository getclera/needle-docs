Switching applicant tracking systems shouldn't mean starting from zero. We run every migration together with your team. There's no self-serve import, so start by reaching out.

<Card title="Email the team" icon="mail" href="mailto:support@needle.so?subject=ATS%20migration">
  Write to [**support@needle.so**](mailto:support@needle.so?subject=ATS%20migration). If your team shares a Slack channel with us, post there instead.
</Card>

## What helps us most

Access to your current ATS's API makes the move fastest and most complete. If that isn't possible, we work from whatever your system can export.

## What can come over

What we can bring over depends on your current system. Usually that includes:

- **Jobs**, with their descriptions, locations, and employment types.
- **Pipeline stages**, built per job from your real interview process, not generic defaults. You approve them before we create them.
- **Candidates**, with contact details, LinkedIn URL, source, and their current stage.
- **CVs** and **application form answers**.
- **Notes** and **interview feedback**, with their original authors and dates, so the timeline reads the same.
- **Archived candidates**, with their archive reasons.

After the import, careers pages arrive unpublished for you to review. Candidate profiles are completed from CVs and LinkedIn, and we propose [screening](/screening) criteria for each job.

## Good to know

- **Email templates and automations** often can't be exported. Show us the ones you use, and we set them up on the matching pipeline stages.
- **Teammates** don't carry over as accounts. Invite your team to Needle. Notes keep the original author's name.
- **Open jobs move first**, closed jobs after, so live hiring isn't held up.
- **Still hiring in your old system during the switch?** We re-run the import to pick up what changed, without duplicating candidates.

## What to send us

- **Your current ATS**, and whether you can give us API access.
- **What you want to bring over**, for example open jobs only, or your full history.
- **Your timeline**, including any date your current contract ends.
- **Who on your team** we should work with.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Kickoff call">
    We agree on what moves, in what order, and how your pipeline stages should look in Needle.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import">
    We move your open jobs first, then closed jobs, while your hiring keeps running.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check together">
    We compare job, candidate, and application counts with your old system. You review everything in Needle before you switch the old system off.
  </Step>
</Steps>
