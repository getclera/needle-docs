## Coming from Ashby

We've moved several teams from Ashby and have a tested import for it. All we need from you is an **Ashby API key** with read access to your jobs, candidates, and interviews. Create one in Ashby's admin settings and share it with us securely.

### What comes over

- **Jobs**: the exact job description from your Ashby posting, plus location and employment type.
- **Pipeline stages**: built per job from your real Ashby interview plan, not generic defaults. We show you the proposed stages before creating them.
- **Candidates**: contact details, LinkedIn URL, source, and their current stage in each job.
- **CVs**: every resume stored in Ashby.
- **Application forms**: your careers-page questions, and each applicant's answers on their profile.
- **Notes**: candidate notes with their author and original date, so the timeline reads the same.
- **Interview feedback**: past feedback and scorecards appear as completed interviews, linked to the stage they happened in.
- **Archived candidates**, with their archive reasons.
- **Interview prep**: question lists and guidance go into each stage's interview preparation.

### What we set up after the import

- **Careers pages** are imported unpublished. You review them, then publish.
- **Candidate profiles** are completed from their CVs and LinkedIn, so company and current role are filled in.
- **[Screening](/screening) criteria** are proposed for each job, and applicants waiting for review are evaluated against them.

### Good to know

- **Email templates and stage automations** aren't available through Ashby's API. Show us the ones you use, and we add them to each stage's follow-up actions.
- **Teammates** don't carry over as accounts. Invite your team to Needle. Notes keep the original author's name.
- **Dropdown and date questions** on application forms become free-text questions in Needle.
- **Custom candidate fields** are kept on the candidate profile, but not as separate Needle fields.
- **Open jobs move first**, closed jobs after, so live hiring isn't held up.
- **Still hiring in Ashby during the switch?** We re-run the import to pick up what changed. Candidates are matched by email, then by LinkedIn URL, so nobody is duplicated.

## Coming from another ATS

Greenhouse, Lever, Workable, Personio, or something else: email us anyway. We'll look at what your current system can export and agree with you on what moves.

## What to send us

- **Your current ATS**, and for Ashby, the API key.
- **What you want to bring over**, for example open jobs only, or your full history.
- **Your timeline**, including any date your current contract ends.
- **Who on your team** we should work with.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Kickoff call">
    We agree on what moves, in what order, and how your pipeline stages should look in Needle.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import">
    We move your open jobs first, then closed jobs, while your hiring keeps running.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check together">
    We compare job, candidate, and application counts with your old system. You review everything in Needle before you switch the old system off.
  </Step>
</Steps>
